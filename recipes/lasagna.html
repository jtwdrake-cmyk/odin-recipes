Hearty Bolognese Sauce Lasagna

When you really want to feel like your’e at an Italian nonna's house, this recipe will be there to give you that warm home cooked feeling.

This recipe uses only the freshest ingredients, with everything made from scratch (even the pasta sheets!) you know it's going to taste good!

Ingredients

  • 1 brown onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
  • 1 clove of garlic, crushed but not chopped
  • 1 can of whole pomodoro tomatoes
  • 400g minced beef and pork mix
  • Red wine
  • 2 Bay leaf
  • 500ml milk
  • 4-5 peppercorns
  • 20g Butter
  • 75g flour
  • Shredded cheese for topping
  • Pasta sheets (See our home made egg pasta recipe here.)

Steps

  1. In a heavy bottomed pan, fry the onions over medium heat until they start to turn translucent. Adding some salt will help to draw out some of the water and they will caramelise more.

  2. Add the minced meat and garlic to the onions. Brown the meat whilst stirring occasionally

  3. Once your meat is browned, add the red wine to the pan. deglaze the bottom of the pan with a wooden spatula, then reduce the liquid to around 1/3 of the original volume.

  4. Once the liquid has reduced, add in your tomatoes and of of your bay leaves. use the spatula to crush the tomatoes in the pan, we want to keep some texture to them, so don't crush them too much.

  5. turn down the heat to a low/medium, and let it cook for around an hour. Stir once in a while to make sur nothing burns onto the bottom of the pan.

  6. in a separate pan, place your milk, crushed garlic clove,remaining bay leaf, and peppercorns. Put the pan on to a low simmer for around 5 to 10 minutes, but do not allow it to boil over.

  7. Melt your butter in a small pan, add the flour and mix it together to create a smooth paste.

  8. Take the milk off of the heat and remove the bay leaf, peppercorns, and garlic clove.

  9. Stir the flour and butter paste into the milk, being careful not to leave any clumps. return the milk to a low heat and stir constantly until the mixture thickens. Make sure to stir the bottom of the pan so that the flour doesn't stick and burn.

  10. Preheat your oven to 180C

  11. In a large dish, make layers of the tomato meat sauce, the white sauce, and the pasta sheets. the final layed should finish with a thick layer of white sauce on top of the meat sauce.

  12. Sprinkle your favorite cheese on the top, then move the dish into the top shelf of the oven.

  13. Cook for 40m, ensuring that the cheese is nicely melted and browned a little on the top.

  14. Bon apetite, you have fiished making this delicious and hearty lasagna!
