Easy Overnight Focaccia

The easiest no knead focaccia recipe

This recipe is as simple as it's possible to be. No kneading needed!

Using the simplest ingredients and minimum effort, you can out together a beatiful tasty bread, perfect for soaking up sauce leftover from a hearty bolognese or lasagna.

Ingredients

Dough

  • 60g Olive oil (split into 2 portions of 35g and 25g)
  • 340g warm water
  • 8g table salt
  • 420g all purpose flour
  • 1 tablespoon instant yeast

Topping

  • 13g olive oil
  • Rosemary, Thyme, or your favorite herbs

Steps

  1. Grease a pan 9" x 13" pan. Drizzle over 25g of olivee oil. The spray keeps the pan non stick, and the olive oil gives flavor and crunch to the bottom.

  2. Weigh out the flour, add to it the rest of your dough ingredients. If you have a stand mixer, beat at high speed for 60s. if doing by hand, mix until you have a sticky thick dough. make sure there are no clumps of flour left.

  3. Scoop the sticky batter into the prepared pan from step one, cover the pan, then let it rise at room temperature for 60 minutes. When it is nice and puffy, it should be ready.

  4. Whilst the dough is rising, preheat the oven to 190C.

  5. Use your finger to poke holes all over the top of the dough.

  6. Drizzle lightly with olive oil, sprinkle with salt, and add your favorite herbs.

  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes.

  8. Remove it from the oven, wait 5 minutes, then turn it out of the pan onto a rack. Serve warm or at room temperature.
